# Look

Newsprint page on a dark desk. Masthead strip at the top of every page (small caps label left,
red "extra" tag right, double rule below). Headlines type in with a caret. Photos are real app
screenshots in a thin black frame with a paper-tape corner and a small italic caption. Red ink is
reserved for the rubber stamp, kickers and underline/circle marks. Motion: page push between
frames, stamps slam in (scale down, slight rotation), photos drop in with a small tilt.
