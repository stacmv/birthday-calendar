## Intent

Promo for "Календарь юбилеев" / "Jubilee Calendar" (anniversary-calendar.hitty-missy.ru). Concept 4, "Числа говорят сами": no phone, no feature list - pure kinetic typography. Numbers are characters: 11 111 shows off, 12 345 lines up like a staircase, 12 321 mirrors itself, 10 000 stays solemnly silent, 300 000 hours drops in. Short captions name what they are (days since birth / hours). Late minimal text beat: enter a birth date -> see your own; it remembers; add to your calendar; works offline. Finale: "У каждого есть свои. Найдите их." / "Everyone has their own. Find yours." + CTA with app icon.
Autoplays MUTED: every message reads from on-screen text. No voice-over. Music: playful minimal plucks (host tool; fall back to silent if unavailable).
One composition, language via variable `lang` ("ru"|"en", default "ru"); all copy from in-frame dictionaries.

## Assets

- assets/icon-512.png - app icon, used at the CTA only.
- assets/fonts/inter-latin.woff2, inter-cyrillic.woff2 - Inter variable (Latin + Cyrillic).

## Notes

Allowed claims only (see shared brief): date of birth -> jubilees in days/hours; round numbers; beautiful numbers; remembers date on device; .ics + Google Calendar export; installable, works offline. No "free", no counts, no ratings.
