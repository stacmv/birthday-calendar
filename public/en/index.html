Jubilee Calendar

When will you turn 15,000 days or 400,000 hours old? Enter your date of birth and see your next life jubilees.

Open the app Install on your phone

Installation is offered on the app page.

What it does

  • Jubilees in days and hours

    The nearest round numbers: hundreds and thousands of days, hundreds and thousands of hours since birth, with dates. The time of birth is optional.

  • Beautiful numbers

    Optionally, the list also shows the nearest beautiful numbers: repeated digits, sequences, palindromes. Off by default.

  • Remembers your date

    Enter it once: the next time you open the app, the calendar appears right away. Change or forget the date in one tap.

  • Calendar export

    Tick the jubilees and download an .ics file or add an event to Google Calendar. The last event in the file is a link that fetches the next jubilees.

  • Works offline

    Install it on your phone and open it without a connection. Your data stays on your device.

  • Themes and two languages

    System, light, dark and high-contrast themes. Russian and English.
